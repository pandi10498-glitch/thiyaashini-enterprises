About Thiyaashini Enterprises

Empowering Farmers With Pure Nature

Under the dedicated leadership of Director Mrs. Murugeswari, Thiyaashini Enterprises operates from Tenkasi, Tamil Nadu, with a steadfast commitment to high-yield, regenerative organic agriculture.
